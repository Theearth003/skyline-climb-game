PublicTrace
Public web only

Find the public trail.

Search public web references to a phone number, or reverse-search an image for copies, similar images, and webpages. It does not expose private WhatsApp activity or hidden account data.

Public phone-number search

Enter a number such as 08105106615. PublicTrace creates focused searches for the open web and major social platforms.

Only publicly indexed pages are searched. A result does not prove that the number belongs to a particular person, and WhatsApp last-seen/online status is not exposed by this tool.

Reverse-image search

Upload an image and send it directly to Google Lens. Lens can return similar images and websites containing the image or a similar image.

This finds web-image evidence, not a guaranteed identity match. Treat similar faces/images as leads to verify, not proof that two accounts are the same person.
PublicTrace is separate from Genie and the Instagram monitor. It is designed for lawful public-information research.
